# E: Small ideas (mini-specs)

Each idea was checked against `index.html` (grep only) and the README. None exists in the page today. All of them use only Richard's own library and the page's own records. Nothing is suggested by Spotify.

## E1. Play buttons on Home's Pinned cards

Pinned items are Richard's favorites, but on Home a pinned card only opens its list, so playing one takes two taps (open, then Play). This adds the same round play button that Jump back in cards already have (`.cardplay` in `cardStrip`) to the Pinned strip, while the card itself still opens the list. In `cardStrip`, the button is added for pinned playlists, albums and artists and calls `onItemClick` with a play-only flag, so `playBody` sends `{context_uri: item.uri}` (the artist context plays the artist, as the artist page's Play does). For a pinned podcast, the button resumes the newest unfinished episode found in data the page already holds: the Latest cache (`state.latest.cache`), the saved show list (`slGet`) and the episode history (`epHistory`), picked the same way as `firstUnplayed`. If none of those has an episode for that show, the podcast card gets no button, and no catalog request is sent. Asleep and wake-mode handling stay as they are, because the tap goes through the existing `play` path. On iPhone, Richard checks that the button is easy to hit without opening the card by mistake, that a pinned album starts at track 1, that a pinned artist plays, and that with Spotify asleep the "Press play in Control Center" line appears and the pin then plays.

Effort: S · Requests: 1 play call per tap (player); none to show the buttons · New scope: no

## E2. Continue where I left off in an album or playlist

When Richard opens an album, a playlist or Liked Songs he was partway through, he has to find the song by hand. This adds a third button under Play and Shuffle in the song list header (`renderDetail`), reading **Continue · "Song name"**, that starts the last song he heard in that list, inside the list, so the following songs keep going. It needs no new requests: whenever the player check (`setPlayer`) sees a song playing with an album, playlist or Liked Songs context (the same test as `jumpCtx`), the page writes `{song uri, name, positionMs, at}` under that context URI in a new localStorage key (for example `lib-ctx-last`), capped at 40 contexts and 30 days. When Recently played is loaded for Home or Recent, its items (which carry `context`) fill gaps the same way. The button calls `play` with `ctx` set to the list's URI, the song as the offset and `positionMs`, which `playBody` already supports. It is hidden when there is no entry, when that song is playing now, or when the saved spot is within the last 30 seconds of the song (then it would just end). On iPhone, Richard checks that the button appears after playing part of a playlist and coming back later, that it continues on the right song and spot, and that the next song after it is the playlist's next song (not shuffled, unless shuffle is on in Spotify).

Effort: S · Requests: none to build the list; 1 play call per tap (player) · New scope: no

## E3. Playlists and Liked Songs in Jump back in

Jump back in shows one card per album from Recently played, even when Richard was actually listening to a playlist or to Liked Songs, so his most played playlists never appear there. This changes `loadRecent` so that a recently played song whose context is one of Richard's library playlists (found with `libraryPlaylist(id)`, which reads the library cache) or Liked Songs (`isLikedCtx`) makes a card for that playlist or for Liked Songs instead of for the album. Each playlist gets one card, placed by its newest play, mixed with album and podcast cards as now by `byTime`. The card opens the playlist's song list, and its round play button plays the last song heard inside that playlist, which the existing `ctx` already does. A playlist that is not in Richard's library is skipped, so the page never calls `playlistById` here and sends no catalog request. Recently played is already fetched at most once every 15 minutes on Home, so nothing new is asked of Spotify. On iPhone, Richard checks that after playing a playlist it appears in Jump back in as the playlist (with its own cover), that the play button continues inside it, and that albums he played directly still show as albums.

Effort: S · Requests: none (reuses the Recently played answer the page already loads) · New scope: no

## E4. Add the playing song to one of my playlists

Today the page can like a song but cannot put it in one of Richard's playlists. This adds an **Add to playlist** button to the Now Playing screen's options (`npOptions`) for songs only. It opens a sheet like the device sheet (`openSheet`) listing Richard's own and collaborative playlists from the library cache, pinned playlists first, with the name filter on top. Tapping one sends one request, `POST /playlists/{id}/items?uris=<song uri>`, which Spotify's February 2026 changelog lists as the replacement for the removed `/tracks` endpoint. The page's `coolGroup` already puts a non-GET `/playlists` call in the **library** group, so it never touches the catalog quota. A toast says **Added to "Playlist"**. If that playlist's songs were already loaded this visit and the song is in it, the sheet asks first ("Already in this playlist. Add again?"); otherwise it does not check, because checking would cost catalog requests. There is no Undo, because Spotify's remove call takes out every copy of the song. This needs the `playlist-modify-private` scope for private playlists (`playlist-modify-public` is already in `SCOPE_LIST`), so the **New features need one more sign-in** banner appears once and Richard must sign in again. Unverified: the changelog does not say whether Development Mode apps may add items, and one Spotify community thread reports a 403 when creating a playlist in Development Mode, so the first test decides whether this ships. Only Richard can check on iPhone that the sign-in works, that the song lands in the right playlist in the Spotify app, and that a private playlist accepts it.

Effort: M · Requests: 1 POST per add (library group) · New scope: yes (playlist-modify-private, forces one re-sign-in)

## E5. Remove an episode from Continue listening

Continue listening keeps any episode Richard started and left, so a show he gave up on stays at the top of Home and Podcasts until he finishes it. Spotify has no way for apps to mark an episode as played, so this is local only. A small **×** button on each Continue listening card (`renderContinue`) hides that episode and shows a toast with **Undo** for a few seconds, like the heart's Undo. The page stores `{uri: positionMs at hide time}` in a new localStorage key (for example `lib-cl-hidden`), up to 60 entries, dropped after 60 days like the episode history. `continueItems` leaves out a hidden episode unless its position has moved more than a minute past the saved one, which means Richard played it again, and then the entry is removed. Latest episodes, the episode list and Recent are not changed, and nothing is sent to Spotify. Diagnostics can show the count next to pins and queued songs. On iPhone, Richard checks that the × is easy to hit without starting the episode, that Undo brings the card back, and that playing a hidden episode again in the Spotify app makes it return once the page sees it playing.

Effort: S · Requests: none · New scope: no
