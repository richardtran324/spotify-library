# My Library — Spotify library page for iPhone

A one-file web page (`index.html`) that lists your Spotify playlists, saved albums, followed artists, saved podcasts, recently played songs and Liked Songs, with no Home feed and no banners. You can also like and save items, queue songs and episodes, open song and episode lists, control playback and choose the device.

- **Tap an item:**
  - **Albums, playlists (yours and other people's), Liked Songs, Saved episodes and podcasts** open their song or episode list. **Play** (and **Shuffle** for song lists) sits at the top of that list, so playing is one more tap. Spotify doesn't let personal apps list the songs in other people's playlists; those open with a short note and **Play**.
  - **Artists** open the artist page (see **Artist page** below).
  - **Songs and episodes** play. Inside an album or playlist a song plays in that album or playlist, so the next songs keep going; an episode picks up where you left off.
  - This works the same on Home, in every list, in Search Spotify results and in Pinned.
- **Spotify already open on a device:** playing starts there, and you stay on the page.
- **Spotify not running:** the page remembers what you asked to play. Press play in Control Center or on the lock screen and it starts as soon as Spotify wakes, without opening the app (see **When Spotify isn't running** below).

Nothing secret is stored in the file. Your sign-in stays in Safari on your phone. Spotify's Client ID identifies your app; it isn't a password.

## Setup (about 10 minutes, once)

### 1. Put the page on GitHub Pages (on your PC)

1. Sign in at github.com (create a free account if you don't have one).
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**. Name it `spotify-library`, choose **Public**, click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**, drag in `index.html` together with `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `manifest.webmanifest` (the Home Screen icon), click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**, **Branch** to `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
5. Wait a minute and refresh. GitHub shows your address, like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/spotify-library/`.

### 2. Register your app with Spotify

1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and sign in with your Spotify account. Accept the developer terms if asked.
2. Click **Create app**:
   - **App name:** My Library
   - **App description:** Personal library page
   - **Redirect URIs:** your GitHub Pages address from step 1.5, exactly, including the `https://` and the final `/`. Click **Add**.
   - **Which API/SDKs:** tick **Web API**.
   - Tick the terms box and click **Save**.
3. Open the app → **Settings**. You'll need the **Client ID** (32 letters and numbers) in the next step. Don't copy the Client secret; the page doesn't use it.

### 3. Sign in on your iPhone

1. Open your GitHub Pages address in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. Check that the address shown on the setup screen matches the Redirect URI you entered.
3. Paste the Client ID and tap **Save and sign in with Spotify**. (To get the Client ID onto the phone, open the Spotify dashboard in Safari on the phone and copy it there.)
4. Approve access on Spotify's page. You return to your library.

Optional: Share → **Add to Home Screen**. The page brings its own icon (`apple-touch-icon.png`) and name. A Home Screen icon keeps its own storage, so you'll sign in once more there. If taps from the Home Screen icon don't open Spotify, use the page in a Safari tab instead.

## Features

- **Filters:** **Home** (the first chip and the starting view; see **Home** below), Playlists (with Liked Songs), Albums, **Artists** (artists you follow; tap one to open its artist page), **Podcasts** (see below), **Recent** (recently played songs from the page's listening log, each song once per day, mixed with podcast episodes from the page's own history (see **Podcast listening history**), newest first under day headings; songs reload each time you pick it; see **Listening log**), All, and Search Spotify. The box above filters the current list by name. The page remembers the filter you picked last; if you never picked one, it opens on Home.
- **Open a pasted link (v2.36):** paste (or type) a Spotify link into the name box, in any filter including Search Spotify: an `open.spotify.com` link (with or without `/intl-xx/`, `?si=…` or `/embed/`) or a `spotify:` URI (`spotify:track:…`, `spotify:episode:…`, `spotify:album:…`, `spotify:playlist:…`, `spotify:artist:…`, `spotify:show:…`). The link must start the box or follow a space (a link tucked inside another link doesn't count). The page shows it under **From your link** instead of filtering or searching by name.
  - **Songs and episodes** get one row; tap it to play, the same way as any other row (one play request, or the usual wait while Spotify is asleep). If the page already knows the song (your library, search results, Recent, an open list or Now Playing), the row shows its name; otherwise it says **Song from link** (or **Episode from link**), and **Show song details** asks Spotify once for the name (remembered until you reload). Playing doesn't need it.
  - **Albums, artists and podcasts** open their page right away, with the same requests as opening them anywhere else. If the album or podcast isn't in your library or pins, its page is titled **Album from link** (or **Podcast from link**), since Spotify's song list doesn't include the name; an artist's page shows the real name once its picture loads, and its pin button appears then (nothing can be pinned under a "from link" name). **Playlists** in your library open at once; any other playlist is looked up first (one request), like **Open playlist** on the Now Playing screen.
  - **Back** returns to the link; pressing **Done** (or **Search**) on the keyboard opens it again. Clear the box to get your list or search back.
  - Short `spotify.link` links can't be opened (the page would need to follow Spotify's redirect); the page says so and offers **Open in Spotify**. Links to anything else (Liked Songs, audiobooks, profiles, other sites such as YouTube) show a short message and are never searched by name.
  - The page never reads the clipboard on its own; paste into the box. Nothing is stored on the phone.
- **Compact view (v2.51):** more of the list fits on screen.
  - The filter chips sit in one row that scrolls sideways instead of wrapping onto a second row. The chip you picked is always scrolled into view (including Search Spotify, the last one).
  - Scrolling down a list (including a song list) slides the **Library** title row and the status line out of view; the search box and chips stay. Scrolling up, or getting near the top, brings them back. They stay put while you're typing (the keyboard is up), and they always show while the wake message ("Press play in Control Center…", "Spotify didn't wake up yet") is up. With **Reduce Motion** on, they appear and disappear instantly instead of sliding.
  - **Compact player** (**Diagnostics → Settings**, off by default): the Now Playing bar becomes one row: cover, title, **Play/Pause** and **Next**. Up next, the heart, Shuffle, Previous and Repeat stay on the full **Now Playing** screen (tap the title). The space kept free at the bottom of the page, and toasts, follow the shorter bar. While Spotify is asleep, the bar's message is cut to one line; the full message is on the Now Playing screen. The setting is stored only on this phone; if Safari won't store it, it applies until you reload and the page says so.
  - None of this sends anything to Spotify.
- **Buttons on each row** (the row itself opens or plays the item, as above):
  - **Heart:** like a song, or save an album or someone else's playlist. A filled heart means it's in your library. Removing something shows **Undo** for a few seconds. Your own playlists, artists and Liked Songs have no heart.
  - **+:** add a song to the queue. Spotify must be open on a device.
  - There's no **›** button any more (v2.12): the row itself opens albums, playlists, podcasts and artists.
- **Pin:** the pin button on playlist, album, artist and podcast rows (in your library and in search results) keeps that item in a **Pinned** section at the top of its own filter (Playlists, Albums, Artists, Podcasts → Shows) and of All, whatever the sort; the rest of the list sorts as before. Tap it again to unpin. Up to 30 pins. Pins are stored only on this phone (in this browser) and are never sent to Spotify.
- **Song list:** shows the cover, **Play**, **Shuffle**, and the songs, 50 at a time (**Load more** for the rest). Under the name, a line gives the total once everything is loaded, such as **12 songs · 48 min** or **1 hr 5 min**; while more pages remain it says **50 of 230 songs loaded** instead (no length, since it would cover only part of the list). Tapping a song plays it in that album or playlist, so the following songs keep going. **Continue · song name (v2.23)** under Play and Shuffle starts the last song you heard in that album, playlist or Liked Songs, at the spot you left it, inside the list, so the next songs keep going. The page remembers it from what the Now Playing check saw (and from Recently played), for up to 40 lists and 30 days, only on this phone. The button is hidden while that song is playing, and when you were in its last 30 seconds. **Back** (or the phone's back swipe) returns to where you were in the list. Spotify only lets personal apps list the songs in playlists you own; for other playlists the page shows a note and you can still press Play. For a playlist you don't own, **Make a copy** shows how to copy it in the Spotify app; the page then lists your copy's songs, links it to the original on this phone, and says when the original has changed.
- **Instant albums and album details (v2.38):** Spotify's answer for your saved albums (loaded with the library anyway) can include each album's first page of songs. When it does, opening a saved album shows its songs at once, even the first time and even while Spotify has paused album details, with no extra request, and **Load more** asks Spotify for the rest of a longer album. These songs are kept only in memory for the visit until you open the album; it's then saved on the phone like any album (**Saved on this phone**). Because the library answer brings these songs back on every load, such albums are the first to go when the 150 saved albums are full, so albums that cost a request to load stay; once **Load more** adds a page, an album counts like any other. An album already saved on the phone still opens from its saved copy. If Spotify leaves the songs out, albums open as before. **Diagnostics** says **Album songs with the library answer: yes, for 48 of 50 saved albums** (or **no**), plus how many came with ℗/© lines. At the end of an album's song list, a footer shows **Released March 3, 2017** (or **March 2017**, or **2017**, as precisely as Spotify knows it), the album's © and ℗ lines, and the label if Spotify sends one (Spotify stopped sending labels in Feb 2026, so usually not). Albums from search or an artist page that aren't saved show the date only. Albums with more than one disc get **Disc 1**, **Disc 2** headers (in album order; not while sorted or filtered). Explicit songs show a small **E** badge before the artist, in every song list, search results, Recent and Up next (Spotify's "not explicit" also covers "unknown", so a missing E isn't a guarantee). Albums saved on the phone before v2.38 get their E badges from the library's songs when Spotify sends them (no request); otherwise after **Reload from Spotify**. If the phone's storage is too full to keep the library itself, Diagnostics says so.
- **Editing your playlists (v2.26):** in a playlist you own or collaborate on, **Edit** above the songs shows them in playlist order with **−** (remove, after a confirm; if a song is in the playlist more than once, Spotify removes every copy) and up and down arrows (move one place). Songs move only among those loaded so far; **Load more** brings in the rest. If the list was loaded over a minute ago and you haven't changed it since opening it, **Edit** first loads its first page again (one request), so a move can't hit the wrong song after the playlist changed elsewhere; while Spotify has paused updates the arrows stay off until you tap **Done** and **Edit** again later (**−** still works). On the Now Playing screen, **Add to playlist** puts the playing song or episode at the end of one of your own or collaborative playlists (pinned ones first, with a name filter); it asks first only if that playlist's songs were already loaded this visit and include it. There's no Undo. The first time you edit, the page asks for one more Spotify permission (**Manage your private playlists**) and Spotify asks you to sign in again; if you cancel there, nothing changes. Each change is one request.
- **Find in list (v2.22):** a **Find in songs** box under the header of Liked Songs, your own playlists, albums and Saved episodes. It matches the song title, artist and album (an episode's show), ignoring capitals and accents ("beyonce" finds Beyoncé), and looks through the songs loaded so far; typing sends nothing to Spotify. Tapping a match plays it inside the list, as usual. While more pages remain, **Search all 1,240 songs** loads the rest: for Liked Songs and Saved episodes that's one request per 50 songs, two at a time, up to 1,000 songs per tap (the button then says **Search 1,000 more**; tap again for the rest), kept until you reload the page; for your own playlists and long albums the button says how many requests it needs, and it's off above 4 (200 more songs) or while Spotify has paused album details, since those requests are limited to about 20 an hour. Any Spotify pause, **Stop**, or leaving the list stops it and keeps what arrived. Clear the box to get the full list and **Load more** back.
- **Artist page:** the artist's picture and name, a **Play** button (plays the artist in Spotify, as tapping an artist used to), a **Follow** button and the pin button beside it, and the artist's releases in three groups, **Albums**, **Singles & EPs** and **Compilations**, each newest first. Releases load 10 at a time (Spotify's limit); **Load more** adds the next 10 and puts them in their groups. Under the name, a line says **10 of 37 releases loaded**, or **37 releases** once all are in. Tap a release to open its song list; **Back** returns to the artist page at the same spot, and **Back** again to where you started. There are no top songs or related artists: Spotify no longer gives personal apps top songs, and the page shows nothing Spotify suggests. **Follow** (v2.25) follows the artist on Spotify and reads **Following** once you do; tap **Following** to unfollow. Search Spotify shows a heart on artist rows that does the same. Each tap shows **Undo** for a few seconds, and the **Artists** filter changes at once. The first time, Follow asks for one more Spotify permission (**Manage who you are following**): a sheet explains it, **Sign in to Spotify** asks Spotify, and you come back to the page; if you cancel there, nothing changes. The button is hidden while your followed artists haven't loaded.
  - **Go to artist (v2.19):** in an album's song list, the artist names under the title are buttons that open the artist page; **Back** returns to the album. The Now Playing screen has **Open artist · …** too (see below). An artist you follow costs one request (its releases), since its picture is already known; any other artist costs two (picture and releases). Opening the same artist again this visit sends nothing.
  - **Liked songs (v2.31):** under the artist's name, a **Liked songs · 12** row lists the songs in your Liked Songs where this artist is one of the credited artists (featured artists count; a different artist with the same name doesn't). It's built from the Liked Songs the page has already loaded this visit, so showing it and opening it send nothing. Tap it for the list, newest liked first, with **Play** and **Shuffle**; tapping a song plays from it through the rest of the list, then wraps round to the songs before it. These play just those songs (the newest 100), not inside Liked Songs, so Now Playing shows no list for them, and if Spotify goes to sleep, resuming plays only the song you were on. A song you like on this page later in the visit makes the row say **so far** again until Liked Songs is loaded once more. If only part of Liked Songs is loaded, the row says **Liked songs · 3 so far** and **50 of 1,240 Liked Songs loaded**, and the list offers **Find the rest**. If none of it is loaded yet, the row says **Find Nova in your Liked Songs**. Both open Liked Songs with **Find in list** filled in with the artist's name, where **Search all** loads the rest as usual (see **Find in list**); **Back** then returns to the artist page with the full count. The row is hidden once the whole list is loaded and has no songs by the artist. Nothing is stored on the phone for it.
- **Now Playing bar:** at the bottom while something plays: cover, song, artist, device, an **Up next** button, a heart for the current song, and Shuffle, Previous, Play/Pause, Next and **Repeat**. It checks every 20 seconds while the page is open on screen (every 60 seconds once nothing has played for 10 minutes), and stops checking when you switch away.
  - **Tap the cover or song name** to open the full **Now Playing** screen (see below).
  - When Spotify goes to sleep after you pause, the bar stays, shows the song as paused, and says **Spotify is asleep. Press play to wake it.** (see **When Spotify goes to sleep** below).
- **Now Playing screen:** slides up over the page. It shows a large cover, the title, the artist or podcast, a bookmark button and a heart.
  - **Bookmark (v2.32):** the button left of the heart saves the song or episode and the spot it's at, as the progress bar shows it (**Bookmarked “…” at 12:34**). It sends nothing to Spotify. A spot in the last 5 seconds is saved 5 seconds before the end, so the bookmark doesn't just start the next song. You can bookmark the same song or episode more than once. They're listed under **Bookmarks** on Home. Local files have no bookmark button. During an ad, Spotify doesn't say what's playing, so the page shows the previous song as asleep and the button bookmarks that song at the spot where it stopped.
  - **Progress bar** with time played and time left. It moves along on its own between checks. Drag it, or tap anywhere on it, to jump there; the page tells Spotify when you let go.
  - **Shuffle, Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Repeat.** Repeat cycles **off → all** (the album or playlist) **→ one** (this song) **→ off**; it's green while on and shows a small 1 for **one**.
  - **Episodes** also get **Back 15 s** and **Forward 30 s**.
  - **Controls follow what Spotify allows (v2.35):** when Spotify says a control can't be used right now (for example skipping back, seeking or shuffle during some playback), Previous, Next, the progress bar, Back 15 s / Forward 30 s, Shuffle and Repeat are greyed out here and on the bar. Tapping a greyed progress bar, Shuffle or Repeat shows the reason (**Spotify doesn't allow seeking here**) and sends nothing. Previous and Next only look greyed: a tap still asks Spotify (it sometimes says skipping back isn't allowed when it is), and if Spotify refuses you get the usual **Couldn't control playback** message. A line under the controls lists what isn't allowed. Greyed controls stay reachable with VoiceOver (they're marked unavailable, not removed), so it can read the reason. Repeat skips a setting Spotify doesn't allow (for example **off → one** when **all** isn't allowed) and greys out only when no change is allowed. They come back as soon as Spotify allows them again. If Spotify doesn't say, nothing is greyed out; the page reads only Spotify's list of things not allowed right now, so if Spotify ever sends that information in another form, nothing is greyed out either. The same line says **Private session is on.** during a private session, and **An ad is playing.** when Spotify reports an ad. This uses the Now Playing check the page already makes, so no extra requests.
  - **Volume** slider, only when the device lets apps change its volume (an iPhone usually doesn't; a computer or speaker usually does).
  - **Playing on … · Change** opens the device list.
  - Buttons for what applies to what's playing:
    - **Open album · …** (songs): the album's song list.
    - **Open artist · …** (songs, v2.19): the artist page, one button per artist, up to 3, so featured artists get one too.
    - **Open playlist · …** when you're playing from a playlist. Your own playlists open their song list. A playlist that isn't in your library is looked up by Spotify (one request, remembered until you reload); since Spotify only lets personal apps list songs in your own playlists, it opens with the usual note and a **Play** button.
    - **Open Liked Songs** when you're playing from Liked Songs.
    - **Open show · …** (episodes): the podcast's episode list.
    - **Share** (v2.20): opens the iPhone share sheet with the song's or episode's Spotify link, so you can send it in Messages or anywhere else.
    - **Open in Spotify:** the song or episode in the Spotify app.
  - **Up next** (top right) opens the queue (see below).
  - Close with the down arrow at the top left, by swiping down from the top of the screen, or with Escape on a keyboard.
  - While this screen is open and on screen, the page checks what's playing every 10 seconds instead of 20 (every 60 seconds once nothing has played for 10 minutes). Nothing is checked while the page is in the background.
  - **Back** from a song list you opened here returns to where you were, at the same scroll position. If you were already in a song list, the new one replaces it, so **Back** still returns to the list you started from. From an artist page, the song list opens on top of it, so **Back** returns to the artist.
- **Up next:** opens from the Now Playing screen or the bar. It asks Spotify once for the queue each time it opens (**Refresh** asks again) and shows what's playing and what comes next. You can't reorder it or play from it here; Spotify doesn't let apps reorder the queue.
  - Spotify adds its own picks after your queue (autoplay) and doesn't say which songs are which. So the page remembers what you added with **+** on this page during the last 24 hours, and **Hide songs Spotify adds after my queue** (on by default, the switch at the top) shows only those, with the note **Spotify may add its own picks after these; they're hidden.** Turn it off to see everything; later songs may then be Spotify's picks.
  - Songs you added in the Spotify app itself, or more than 24 hours ago, are hidden while the switch is on.
- **Next from and end times (v2.41):** with **Hide songs Spotify adds after my queue** on, **Up next** also shows the songs that follow in the album, playlist or Liked Songs you're playing, in their own section, **Next from · Blue Album** (or the playlist's name, or **Liked Songs**; a podcast shows its next episodes the same way), under your **+** songs. The page picks them by checking each queued song against song lists it already has: saved album copies, album songs that came with your library, saved playlist copies, playlist and album pages loaded this visit, Liked Songs pages you scrolled through this visit, lists open now, and saved podcast copies. It takes them in order and stops at the first song that isn't in the list, which is where Spotify's own picks start (or where the page doesn't know the list), so those stay hidden. It works with shuffle on. If only part of the list is loaded (a long playlist or Liked Songs), the section can stop early and says **Showing the songs this page knows from this list.** Someone else's playlist, or a list the page hasn't loaded, shows no section, as before.
  - A line at the top says how long the shown queue is and when it ends: **5 songs · 18 min · ends about 9:41 PM** (your phone's clock; the time left in the current song is included). While paused it says **paused** instead of an end time. Spotify doesn't say how many songs the queue answer holds (about 20), so when the shown songs run to the end of a full answer it says **20+ songs · at least 1 hr · ends after 9:41 PM**. It's as of when **Up next** opened; **Refresh** updates it.
  - The Now Playing screen shows **Ends 9:41 PM** before the time left while something plays.
  - None of this sends a request: it uses the one queue request **Up next** already makes, and nothing new is saved on the phone (the Liked Songs list is kept in memory for the visit only).
- **Songs that can't play (v2.35):** if Spotify marks a song in a list as unplayable, its row is dimmed and its second line ends with the reason: **Not available in your country**, **Explicit content is off**, **Not available on your plan** or **Not available**. When Spotify says the song can't play, tapping it shows the reason and plays nothing; when Spotify only gives a reason, the row is dimmed but a tap still tries to play it. Album lists saved on the phone keep the reason. Spotify only includes this when a request names a country, which the page's requests don't, so you'll rarely see it.
- **Playing now:** in any song or episode list (albums, playlists, Liked Songs, Recent, search, podcasts), the song or episode that's playing shows its name in green with a small **Playing** label. It updates with the Now Playing check.
- **Keep your place (v2.42):** iPhone Safari often reloads a tab you left in the background. When that happens, the page comes back where you were: the same filter, Podcasts tab (Latest or Shows) and filter text, the album, playlist, podcast or artist page you had open (up to 4 deep, so **Back** still goes artist → list), and the scroll spot of each. Nothing new is asked of Spotify for it: albums and podcasts saved on this phone (see **Saved album and podcast lists**) and other people's playlists come back as they were, and Liked Songs and Saved episodes load their first page as usual (one library request). A saved podcast doesn't check for new episodes on its own after a reload; **Check for new episodes** still does. Anything that would need an album or podcast lookup (your own playlists, an album or podcast not saved on this phone, artist pages) comes back as its header with **Play** and a **Reopen “Name”** button, and asks Spotify only when you tap it. It only happens when the same tab reloads within 6 hours: a new tab, typing the address, the Wake Spotify shortcut (`?wake=1`), a shortcut link (`?play=…`) and the return from signing in all start fresh. It's kept in this tab's session storage only. Diagnostics shows a **Keep place** line (what came back, or why not, and how this page load started).
  - **Each list keeps its spot:** switching Playlists → Albums → Playlists returns to where you were in Playlists (and Latest and Shows keep theirs), until you reload. With text in the filter box, a switch starts at the top. Tapping the filter you're already on scrolls to the top.
  - **Jump to playing:** in a song or episode list, when the playing song or episode is among the rows loaded so far but off screen, a green **Jump to playing** button appears at the bottom right (above the Now Playing bar). It scrolls the row to the middle of the screen and briefly highlights it (no animation with Reduce Motion on). It works in any sort and with Find in list, never loads more pages, and sends nothing to Spotify.
- **Share (v2.20):** albums, playlists, podcasts and artist pages have a share button at the top, next to the heart or pin. It opens the iPhone share sheet with the Spotify link. If the share sheet isn't available, the link is copied instead (**Link copied**); if copying doesn't work either, the message shows the link as text (tap it to select it, then copy) and stays up for 15 seconds or until you tap **Close**. Closing the share sheet does nothing. Liked Songs and Saved episodes have no share button, since they have no public link. Sharing sends no requests to Spotify.
- **Shortcut links (v2.39):** an address like `…/spotify-library/?play=liked` plays something as soon as the page opens, so an iPhone shortcut (Siri, Home Screen, Back Tap) can start it in one step. See **Shortcut links** below.
- **Choosing a device:** tap the device name in the status line ("Playing on iPhone · Change") to see your open Spotify devices. Picking one moves playback there and makes it the device taps play on from then on. **Refresh** in that panel looks again.

### Home

Home is a shortcuts page made only from your own things the page already loads. Nothing on it is suggested by Spotify. Besides recently played, its only extra requests are the episode progress checks described under **Podcasts → Fresh listening progress**. From the top:

- **Liked Songs** as a large row: tap it to open the song list (Play and Shuffle are at its top).
- **Pinned:** everything you pinned (playlists, albums, artists, podcasts), as a row of covers you swipe sideways, in the order you pinned them. A tap does the same as in its own tab: playlists, albums and podcasts open their list, artists open the artist page.
  - **The round play button (v2.23)** on a playlist, album or artist plays it straight away (an album from its first song). On a podcast it picks up the newest episode you haven't finished, from what the page already knows (Latest episodes, the podcast's saved list and your listening history); a podcast the page knows no episodes of yet has no button. Showing the buttons asks Spotify nothing; a tap sends one play request.
- **Continue listening:** the episodes you've started, the same row as on Podcasts. A tap picks the episode up where you left off.
- **Bookmarks (v2.32):** the spots you saved with the bookmark button on the Now Playing screen, newest first, the newest 5 with **Show all** for the rest. Each row shows the song or episode, its artist or podcast and the spot (**at 12:34**). Tap a row to play that song or episode from that spot (one play request, on your chosen device). If Spotify is asleep or not running, it does what any tap does (see **When Spotify isn't running**): with the default **Wait for me to press play** it waits for you to press play in Control Center or on the lock screen; with **Open the Spotify app** or **Run my Wake Spotify shortcut** it opens Spotify (or runs the shortcut) instead, then plays from the spot. The **×** button removes one; **Undo** (for a few seconds) brings back everything you removed while the message was up. Bookmarks are kept only on this phone (in this browser), up to 200 and for up to 2 years; past 200 the oldest go.
- **Jump back in:** your last 8 albums from Recently played (one card per album) and podcasts you listened to (one card per podcast), newest first. Each card shows the album (with its artist) or the podcast (with the newest episode you heard). Since v2.23, songs you played from one of your own library playlists or from Liked Songs show as that playlist or Liked Songs (one card each, with the song you last heard), not as their albums. A playlist that isn't in your library still shows as the album, and nothing is looked up for it.
  - **Tap the card** to open that album's song list or the podcast's episode list.

### Shortcut links (v2.39)

### Spotify's request limit (v2.13, per part since v2.14)

Spotify limits how many requests an app makes. Its documentation says the limit is counted over a rolling 30-second window, and apps in Development Mode (a personal app like this one) also have a separate quota whose numbers Spotify doesn't publish. Going over either one makes Spotify answer **API rate limit exceeded** (HTTP 429) for a while.

**Why v2.14 changed it:** a real Diagnostics report showed only 21 requests in an hour, yet Spotify answered **QUOTA_EXCEEDED**, and every refused request was a catalog lookup (a podcast's episodes, an album's songs, an episode's details). Playback and library requests weren't refused. So the quota appears to be counted per kind of request, and the catalog part ran out first: each Latest episodes reload asked once per saved podcast (about 50 lookups), and the resume-point check asked for single episodes every 10 minutes. v2.13 then paused everything for 20 minutes or more, which made the page unusable. This is the page's reading of one report, not something Spotify documents.

- **Only the part Spotify refused is paused.** Requests are in four groups: **playback** (what's playing, devices, play, pause, queue), **your library** (playlists, saved albums, followed artists, Liked Songs, saved podcasts and episodes, recently played, hearts), **search**, and **podcast and album details** (a podcast's episodes, an album's songs, an artist's releases, a playlist's songs). A 429 pauses only its group. A slim line under the header names it, with a countdown: **Spotify paused podcast and album details until 10:31 PM. Playback, your library and search still work.** Everything else keeps working normally. A tap that needs the paused part sends nothing and shows that message once. The Now Playing screen shows the line only when playback itself is paused. When the countdown ends, playback and library checks start again a few seconds apart; details and search wait for your next tap.
- **How long:** as long as Spotify asks (its Retry-After value). If the page can't read that value, 30 seconds. If Spotify says the quota is used up, at least 10 minutes, then 20, 40, 80 minutes and at most 2 hours if it happens again in the same group. After 2 hours with no quota answer in that group, it starts over at 10 minutes. Spotify doesn't say how long a quota block lasts.
- **A refused request is never sent again on its own,** and identical requests made at the same moment share one answer (opening the same album twice quickly sends one request).
- **Latest episodes refresh only when you open them:** opening **Podcasts → Latest** reloads it only when the saved list is more than 12 hours old (or a reload was cut short by leaving the page), and **Refresh** reloads it at most once per 5 minutes. Each reload asks for at most 12 podcasts, 2 at a time (v2.45: only those with new episodes, plus any not asked for 3 days; when more are due than fit, 3 places, or 6 while some podcast has never been checked, go to the ones checked longest ago), and keeps the other podcasts' episodes as they were. Nothing reloads in the background. Home's **Latest episodes** and the Shows order use the saved list only. Next to **Refresh** it says **Updated 3 h ago**, plus **Paused by Spotify until 10:31 PM** while details are paused.
- **Catalog budget guard (v2.45):** the page counts its podcast and album detail requests over the last hour, shared by every Safari tab on this phone. At 18 (Spotify refused them at about 21), the page's own automatic lookups wait: Latest opening by itself, a saved podcast's 12-hour check and the Now Playing screen looking up a playlist's name. Latest then says **Few album and podcast lookups left this hour, so it waits. Refresh still works.** Your taps still go: **Refresh** in Latest asks as many podcasts as fit under about 21 (at least 2), and opening albums, podcasts and artists works as usual. Diagnostics shows **Album and podcast lookups: 15 in the last hour** and when automatic lookups can go again. The 429 pauses above are unchanged. The page can't see requests from another app using the same Spotify developer account, or from the Home Screen icon (separate storage).
- **No automatic resume-point checks.** **Continue listening** uses the page's own podcast history (what it saw playing) plus the Latest list and any Saved episodes or podcast list you open. Progress you made only in the Spotify app shows up once the page sees that episode playing, or after a Latest reload or opening the podcast.
- **Album, podcast, artist and playlist pages are kept for this visit:** opening the same one again sends nothing. **Refresh** (top right) forgets them, so the next open asks again. (Album and podcast lists are also saved on the phone since v2.15, and your own playlists since v2.37; see **Saved album and podcast lists** below.)
- **Fewer requests to start with:** the Now Playing check runs every 20 seconds (10 with the Now Playing screen open, 60 once nothing has played for 10 minutes, none in the background); waiting for Spotify to wake checks every 3 seconds (Control Center) or 1.5 seconds (Open the Spotify app / shortcut); recently played every 15 minutes; hearts are checked once per item per browser session.
- **Several Safari tabs** share one Now Playing check: only the tab on screen asks Spotify, and the others show its answer. They also share the pause line.
- **Tip: don't run the Safari tab and the Home Screen icon at the same time.** On iPhone the Home Screen icon keeps its own storage, so the two can't share the check or the pause line, and both ask Spotify on their own. Both count against the same app limit. Use one or the other, or close the one you're not using (swipe it away in the app switcher).

### Saved album and podcast lists (v2.15)

Album and podcast details are the part of Spotify's quota that runs out first, so the page saves those lists on the phone and asks Spotify for them as rarely as it can. Nothing is fetched in the background.

- **Albums:** the songs you load in an album are saved (up to 150 albums; the oldest saved goes first). Albums don't change, so opening a saved album sends nothing; it says **Saved on this phone**. If only part of a long album was loaded, **Load more** asks Spotify for the next page and adds it to the saved copy. **Reload from Spotify** (next to **Saved on this phone**) loads it again from the start, unless album details are paused.
- **Podcasts:** the newest 50 episodes of each podcast you open are saved (up to 60 podcasts). Opening a saved podcast shows it right away and asks Spotify for the newest page only when the copy is more than 12 hours old, or when you tap **Check for new episodes**; new episodes go on top. If more new episodes came out than that page holds, the list starts over from the newest page instead, so none are skipped in between. Listening progress the page saw since then is shown over the saved copy. **Load more** asks for older episodes.
- **Playlists (v2.37):** the songs you load in your own and collaborative playlists are saved too, with the playlist's version number from Spotify (its snapshot, which comes with your library list, so checking it costs nothing). Opening a saved playlist sends nothing while that number is the same, and says **Saved on this phone**; when the playlist has changed (in the Spotify app or anywhere else), it loads from Spotify again as before. Only the number from the library loaded since you opened the page (or since the last **Refresh**) counts, so open the page or tap **Refresh** after changing a playlist elsewhere. It opens 50 songs at a time as before; **Load more** takes the next songs from the phone, then from Spotify once the saved ones run out. **Edit** and **Add to playlist** remove that playlist's saved copy (Spotify's new number could also cover changes made elsewhere that the page hasn't seen), so the next visit loads it from Spotify again and saves it; for the rest of the visit the page keeps showing your changes as before. **Reload from Spotify** loads it again from the start. Up to 25 playlists and 1,000 songs each (about 500 KB in all); the one you opened longest ago goes first, and a playlist not opened for 90 days or no longer in your library is removed.
- **From Latest episodes:** a podcast you haven't opened yet, but that has episodes in **Latest**, opens with those episodes right away (**Newest episodes from Latest**) without asking Spotify; **Load more** then asks for its full first page. When **Latest** reloads, a saved podcast copy also gets the new episodes it found, if they join up with the saved ones (otherwise the copy stays as it was, and the next open asks for the newest page).
- **Refresh** (top right) doesn't remove the saved lists. **Diagnostics → Settings** shows **Saved lists: N albums, M shows, P playlists (≈ K KB)** and has **Clear saved lists**. If the phone's storage is full, the page drops the oldest saved lists to make room, or doesn't save.

**While Spotify has paused album and podcast details** (or refuses the request as it's sent):

- **A saved album or podcast** opens as usual. A podcast says **Saved Nov 22 · Spotify paused updates until 10:31 PM**.
- **An album that isn't saved** is found through search instead (search has its own quota): **From search (Spotify paused album details until 10:31 PM)**. The page searches for songs on that album by its first artist, keeps only the songs from this exact album, and lists them in disc and track order. Search can miss songs, so the list may be incomplete; **Load more** looks at the next 10 results, up to 50. Taps play inside the album as usual. If search finds every song, the album is saved like one loaded from Spotify.
- **A podcast that isn't saved** shows **Your saved and played episodes from this show (Spotify paused the full list until 10:31 PM)**: your saved episodes of it (from the newest 50 you saved) plus the episodes the page saw you play, newest first. The page's history has no release date, so a played episode is placed by when you listened. This list isn't saved.

**Diagnostics** shows the request meter: requests from this copy of the page in the last 30 seconds, 10 minutes and hour, requests from all copies in this browser in the last 10 minutes and hour, requests per group (10 minutes / 1 hour), which requests were most frequent, the **Last 429** (time, Spotify's reason, the Retry-After value or **not exposed** when the browser can't read it, and the request), **Paused by Spotify** for each group (working, or paused until when, plus the next quota pause length), a note on what Latest asks, the podcast episode counts (how many are known, how many podcasts Latest would ask now, how many are marked new), **Album and podcast lookups: N in the last hour** (v2.45), and which copy is running the **Player check**.

### No internet and errors in plain words (v2.43)

- **No internet:** when a request gets no answer at all (iPhone Safari calls this "Load failed"), or the phone says it's offline, one quiet line under the header says **No internet. The page will catch up when you're back.** Background checks don't show toasts while it's up, and the Now Playing check slows to once a minute. As soon as Spotify answers again (or the phone says it's back online) the line goes and the page checks once on its own: what's playing, your devices if that check had failed, and your library if it never loaded. Paused parts of the page still send nothing.
- **Coming back to the tab:** the first checks wait about 1 second after you switch back to Safari (iPhone Safari can stall a request sent the instant the page shows, then fail it). If one of those checks fails with "Load failed" within 45 seconds of coming back, it's asked once more 2 seconds later, quietly. Waiting for Spotify to wake still starts right away.
- **Errors say what happened:** **No internet connection**, **Spotify isn't answering right now** (Spotify had a server problem), **Spotify Premium is needed to control playback**, or **That item isn't available in your country**. Other errors keep Spotify's own words in brackets, as before. **Diagnostics** still shows the raw error, plus a **Connection** line.
- **Try again:** the message has a **Try again** button when trying again makes sense (no internet, or Spotify not answering): a play tap, Play, Pause, shuffle, repeat and volume, switching devices, a heart, Follow, opening a playlist, and **Refresh**. If your library couldn't load at all, **Try again** is on the page under the message. Song and episode lists keep their own **Try again** under the list. There's never a **Try again** after Spotify asked the page to slow down (the pause line says when), for Premium or region messages (trying again won't change them), or for **Next**, **Previous**, **Add to queue** and playlist edits (a repeat could skip or add twice), or for a seek (the spot would be out of date). The same message isn't shown twice in a row; its **Try again** then repeats the newest action.
- **Requests:** none extra. A Try again tap sends the one request that failed (a play tap may check your devices first, as a normal tap does). The quiet retry after coming back is one more request only after a failure that got no answer from Spotify.
