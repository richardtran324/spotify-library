    Shortcut links

    A link plays one of your things when an iPhone shortcut opens it. Only your library, pins and the names below play on their own; other links ask first.

      1. Tap Copy link next to what you want.
      2. In the Shortcuts app, tap +, add the action Open URLs and paste the link. Name the shortcut; that’s what you say to Siri. (A Spotify Share link also works after play=, as it is.)
      3. Home Screen: in Shortcuts, tap ⋯ on the shortcut, tap ⓘ (Details), then Add to Home Screen.
      4. Back Tap: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap, pick Double Tap or Triple Tap, then the shortcut.
      5. The link opens in Safari, where you’re signed in. If Spotify is asleep, the page waits as set above (When Spotify isn’t running).
